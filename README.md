## Why Choose Aetherium Over Standard Tweakers?

Most "boost" utilities are blunt instruments—they hammer the registry and disable services, often harming non-game applications. Aetherium Engine takes a **surgical approach**. We believe in *de-cluttering*, not *de-fanging*. Here’s the difference:

- **Context-Aware CPU Core Parking:** We don't disable core parking entirely; we just make it *smarter*. The engine resizes the parked core pool based on the foreground window title.
- **Memory Latenator:** Instead of forcing a non-sensical high priority, we align your system's memory pages with the game's executable signature, reducing page faults without infringing on OS stability.
- **User-Space Priority Inversion:** We don't force "High" priority on games. We simply lower the timer resolution of background processes, giving your game a smoother share of the CPU quanta without blocking background audio or chat apps.
